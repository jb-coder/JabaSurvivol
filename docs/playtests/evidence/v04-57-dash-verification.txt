# v0.4.0 Slice 57 Dash and Shockwave Verification

Automated verification for Dash, Shockwave, and active-skill selection.

## Core

- Zero-rank and passive nodes cannot be selected.
- Allocated active nodes can be selected and persist.
- Validation falls back when the selected skill loses its rank.
- Dash distance, cooldown, invulnerability, and rank scaling.
- Shockwave damage, radius, cooldown, and instant resolution.
- Selected skill survives death while run state resets.

## EditMode

- Selection gating and persistence.
- Dash and Shockwave rank-scaled values.

## PlayMode

- Dash moves the player by the rank-scaled distance.
- Dash ignores damage during its invulnerability window and takes damage after it.
- Shockwave damages and knocks back a nearby zombie.
- Active skill selection works from the character sheet and is saved.
- Zero-rank active skills cannot be selected.

## Android

- ARM64 production APK and ARM64 + x86_64 emulator APK built.
- Emulator smoke test passed on phone and tablet layouts.
