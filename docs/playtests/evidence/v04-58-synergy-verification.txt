# v0.4.0 Slice 58 Synergy Verification

Automated verification for the three skill synergies.

## Core

- Both prerequisite ranks are required for each synergy.
- Berserker adds 10% damage below 50% health.
- Repeated stat aggregation does not double-apply synergy modifiers.
- Ghost grants 10% movement speed for 2 seconds after a kill.
- Ghost does not activate without both ranks.
- Bastion regenerates 2 armor per second after 5 seconds without damage.
- Taking damage resets the Bastion delay.
- Restart resets synergy timers.

## EditMode

- Prerequisite gating.
- Ghost and Bastion run-only state and reset.

## PlayMode

- Berserker increases effective weapon damage below half health.
- Ghost raises move speed after a kill and expires.
- Bastion regenerates armor out of combat.
- The character sheet shows synergy status.

## Android

- ARM64 production APK and ARM64 + x86_64 emulator APK built.
- Emulator smoke test passed on phone and tablet layouts.
- The synergy status row is visible in the character-sheet screenshots.
