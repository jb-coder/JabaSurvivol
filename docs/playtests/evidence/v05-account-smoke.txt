## Automated flow

1. Installed the emulator APK with `adb install -r`.
2. Launched `com.unity3d.player.UnityPlayerActivity`.
3. Confirmed the main menu shows `NOT SIGNED IN - LOCAL PLAY ONLY`.
4. Opened the account panel with a touch tap.
5. Confirmed the account panel shows username, password, register, login, and
   close controls on phone and tablet layouts.
6. Repeated the main menu and account panel checks on the tablet override.
7. Checked logcat for `FATAL EXCEPTION`, `AndroidRuntime` crash, unhandled
   exception, and `NullReferenceException`. None were present.
8. Performed a clean uninstall and reinstall, then confirmed the development
   probe reports `SecureTokenStore probe: ok provider=AndroidKeyStore`
   using the isolated `jaba_survivol_probe` alias. This exercises Android
   Keystore key generation, AES-GCM save, load, compare, and clear.
