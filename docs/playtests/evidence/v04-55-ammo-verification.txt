# v0.4.0 Slice 55 Ammo Verification

Automated verification for ammunition, reload, and weapon switching.

## Core

- Per-weapon magazine and reserve state in `WeaponLoadout`.
- Pistol infinite reserve and magazine consumption.
- Reload timing uses the effective reload speed, including Engineering and upgrades.
- Engineering magazine capacity scaling.
- Shotgun consumes one shell per trigger pull.
- Switching preserves each weapon's magazine.
- Ammo crates fill every finite reserve and ignore the pistol.
- Restart resets magazines and reserves.

## EditMode

- Firing consumption and effective reload time.
- Per-weapon magazine state across switches.
- Ammo crate refill and death reset.

## PlayMode

- Firing consumes ammo and manual reload refills the magazine.
- Empty magazines stop firing and auto reload on the next trigger pull.
- Weapon switching preserves each magazine.
- Death and restart reset the run ammunition.
- Reload is rejected when the magazine is full.

## Android

- ARM64 production APK and ARM64 + x86_64 emulator APK built.
- Emulator smoke test passed on phone and tablet layouts.
- The HUD weapon and ammo panel is visible in `v04-55-playing.png`.
