reported:

```text

High: GHSA-f7vh-h5g3-7jrh
Aspire.Hosting 13.6.4 (transitive)
```

The AppHost SDK was upgraded from `Aspire.AppHost.Sdk/13.6.4` to
`Aspire.AppHost.Sdk/13.6.5`. Re-running the audit reports no vulnerable
packages for the AppHost or any other project.
