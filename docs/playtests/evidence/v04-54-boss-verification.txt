# v0.4.0 Slice 54 Boss and Victory Verification

Automated verification for the round 10 boss and victory flow.

## Core

- Boss catalog values, telegraphed slam data, enrage threshold, and enraged attack interval.
- Boss round stays active after the last regular enemy and waits for the boss.
- Boss spawn is rejected outside round 10 and before the regular enemies are cleared.
- Boss death grants 1,000 supplies and 500 XP once, clears the round, and sets victory.
- Restart resets boss and victory state.
- Death during the boss round ends the run without a victory.

## EditMode

- Boss definition data.
- Boss round gating.
- Victory reward and duplicate rejection.
- Enrage below half health.

## PlayMode

- Boss spawns after the regular enemies and shows the boss HUD.
- Boss kill enters victory, updates the profile, records round 10, and saves.
- Boss enrages and starts the telegraphed slam.
- Death during the boss round keeps persistent supplies.
- Victory rewards are not granted twice.

## Android

- ARM64 production APK and ARM64 + x86_64 emulator APK built.
- Emulator smoke test passed on phone and tablet layouts with no fatal app errors.
- Boss and victory visuals are exercised by the PlayMode suite; the normal menu
  flow does not expose a round 10 shortcut on device.
