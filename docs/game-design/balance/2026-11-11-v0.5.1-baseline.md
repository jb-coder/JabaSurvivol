## Watch items for the beta

These are recorded for #101 rather than changed now, because the current
baseline is inside every target and a numeric change would be speculation.

- **Round 4 brute density.** Round 4 introduces brutes with a flat spawn
  chance and the seed produced five brutes, so round time jumps from 24.55
  seconds in round 3 to 45.60 seconds. A local, uncommitted ramp experiment
  (10-15 percent base chance, plus 5 percent per round, capped at 30 percent)
  reduced the spike to about 35.65 seconds, but it also pushed the default build
  to level 8 and made no synergy reachable. That experiment is not part of this
  change. Composition and economy must be tuned together with tester feedback.
- **Weapon variety.** The simulation strategy buys the rifle and skips the SMG,
  shotgun, and LMG. The simulation does not exercise all five weapons.
- **Active-skill uptime.** The default strategy spends skill points on Strength
  and Vitality, so the selected active skill has rank zero and no uptime. This
  is a strategy assumption, not a balance defect.

## Decision

No gameplay numbers changed in this slice. The deterministic baseline is
healthy, and the guard tests now fail if a future data change moves pacing,
rewards, the boss fight, or ammo pressure outside the target ranges. Numeric
feel tuning is deferred to the five-person beta in #101.
