## Round 4 brute experiment

The baseline note flagged the round 4 brute density as a watch item. Two caps
were tested by replacing excess round 4 brutes with runners:

| Experiment | Round 4 time | Supplies earned | Supplies remaining | XP | Final level | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Cap 2 | 37.40 s | 4820 | 1445 | 3810 | 8 | No weapon damage upgrade; no synergy |
| Cap 3 | 40.50 s | 4835 | 1460 | 3825 | 8 | No weapon damage upgrade |

Both caps smooth the round 4 spike, but they drop the run below level 9 and
remove the first weapon damage upgrade and every synergy. The XP loss is small,
but it crosses a progression threshold. Compensating with a reward or XP change
would affect every round and is not justified without tester feedback.

Decision: no spawn-composition change. The uncapped baseline stays inside every
documented target.

## Weapon scenarios

`BalanceSimulation.RunWeaponScenarios()` runs one full progression per weapon
with every weapon unlocked and the chosen weapon equipped. The default
rifle-first strategy does not replace it.

| Weapon | Run s | Supplies earned | Supplies remaining | Final level | Boss TTK s | Max ammo pressure | Outliers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pistol | 442.19 | 4865 | 1665 | 9 | 14.94 | 0.32 | 2 |
| SMG | 2929.53 | 4865 | 1435 | 9 | 2400.00 | 1.00 | 6 |
| Shotgun | 726.94 | 4865 | 1415 | 9 | 34.36 | 0.42 | 1 |
| Rifle | 616.09 | 4865 | 1515 | 9 | 30.74 | 0.38 | 0 |
| LMG | 389.97 | 4865 | 1440 | 9 | 16.84 | 0.30 | 1 |

The scenario is a sandbox, not a progression claim: every weapon is available
at level 1. It intentionally exposes off-role behavior:

- The SMG starves in the late rounds and cannot kill the boss in the scenario.
  This is consistent with an early-run weapon and is a candidate for #101
  feedback, not a silent stat change.
- The pistol and LMG clear the boss below the boss target when fully unlocked
  at level 1. The pistol is the starting weapon and the LMG is a late unlock;
  the sandbox does not model unlock pacing. The pistol's second outlier is the
  missing weapon unlock, which is expected because the scenario equips it
  without buying another weapon.
- The shotgun has one trivial elite round because its pellet burst exceeds the
  elite shot threshold. This is a scenario threshold artifact, not a P0 outlier.
- The rifle remains clean, which preserves the default progression.

## Active skills

The default simulation strategy still spends skill points on the passive
branches and leaves the selected active skill at rank zero. That is a strategy
assumption, not a balance defect. The existing maxed-profile test confirms that
Turret, Dash, Shockwave, and the three synergies report non-zero uptime when
ranked. Real active-skill usage remains part of the #101 feedback pass.

## Decision

- No gameplay numbers changed in this slice.
- The round 4 cap is rejected because it violates progression targets.
- The weapon scenario harness and rifle guard are added for future tuning.
- The default simulation stays at 0 outliers and inside every target range.
- Five-tester validation remains tracked in #101 and will be folded into this
  note when available.
