# v0.7.1 Audio Review and Fixes

- Release date: 2026-11-14
- Tag: `v0.7.1`
- Milestone: v0.7.1 Audio Review and Fixes
- Branch flow: `release/v0.7.1` -> `main`, then backmerge to `develop`

## Summary

The v0.7.1 release audits and repairs the audio layer. It replaces the
single-source mute path with a pooled audio service, adds persisted master and
effects volume, suspends audio on pause and background, adds the missing combat,
skill, boss, round, and UI cues, and exposes audio controls on the main and pause
menus. Gameplay rules, balance, save data, and persistent progression are
unchanged.

## Included

- Audio audit and cue matrix in `docs/standards/audio.md`.
- A fixed 12-source pool, a global voice limit, and per-cue cooldowns.
- Persisted master mute, master volume, and effects volume with clamping and
  migration from the existing `audio.muted` key.
- Suspend and resume behavior for pause, focus loss, and Android background.
- Thirty-three procedural cues covering weapon fire, empty magazines, reloads,
  weapon switches, impacts, player damage, kills, active skills, turret fire,
  boss spawn, telegraph, slam, enrage and death, round start and clear, reward,
  level-up, victory, defeat, purchases, errors, and UI feedback.
- Mute and master volume controls on the main and pause menus.
- English and Spanish layouts for the audio panel. No new Spanish locale
  strings were added.
- Android emulator evidence in English and Spanish for phone and tablet.

## Known issues and deferred work

- Android evidence is emulator-only. Physical phone and tablet profiling is
  still tracked in #99.
- No human playtest is claimed for this release.
- Procedural cues remain placeholder audio. Final authored music and production
  sound effects remain in v1.0.0.
- The production APK is ARM64-only and was not installed on the x86_64
  emulator.
- The audio panel exposes mute and master volume; the effects volume remains
  internal in v0.7.1.
