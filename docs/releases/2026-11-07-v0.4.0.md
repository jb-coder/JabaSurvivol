# v0.4.0 Content and Boss

- Status: in progress
- Milestone: v0.4.0 Content and Boss
- Tag: created only on `main` at release time

## Scope

- Zombie archetypes and elite rounds.
- Round 10 boss encounter with a telegraphed slam, enrage phase, boss HUD, and victory flow.
- Ammunition, reload, and weapon switching.
- Turret active skill.
- Dash and Shockwave active skills with active-skill selection.
- Skill synergies.
- Extended headless balance simulation for content, boss, ammunition, active skills, and synergies.

## Included so far

- Walker, Runner, and Brute archetypes with deterministic round composition and elite scaling.
- The Warden boss encounter with 2,400 health, a telegraphed 45-damage slam, and an enrage phase below 50% health.
- Boss HUD and victory screen with next-run and main-menu actions.
- Persistent progression remains unchanged on death during the boss round.
- Per-weapon magazines and reserves, manual and automatic reload, weapon switching, and ammo crate reserve refills.
- Turret active skill with rank gating, target acquisition, rank-scaled damage and duration, cooldown, and an active-skill HUD button.
- Dash and Shockwave active skills, active-skill selection from the armory or character sheet, and persistent selection.
