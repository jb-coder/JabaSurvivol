# v0.8.0 Top-Down Art and Arena Upgrade

- Release date: 2026-11-15
- Tag: `v0.8.0`
- Build provenance: APKs built from release commit `20f595c`; the evidence-only follow-up commit does not change runtime code.
- Milestone: v0.8.0 Top-Down Art and Arena Upgrade
- Branch flow: `release/v0.8.0` -> `main`, then backmerge to `develop`

## Summary

The v0.8.0 release replaces the side-view actor read with a top-down actor set,
rebuilds the arena with layered neon environment art, extends the playable arena
beyond the camera view, and adds a following camera with clamped bounds. The
visual-polish post-processing path was evaluated and disabled for this release
because it washed out the 2D scene on the Android emulator; follow-up #203
tracks the corrected Bloom/Vignette implementation.

## Included

- Top-down player, Walker, Runner, Brute, Warden, and turret read with forward
  arms, forward weapons, and stepping legs.
- Thirty new environment sprites: seamless floor variants, decals, props,
  storefronts, fences, barriers, and light pools.
- Dedicated `Environment` atlas with Android ASTC 6x6 compression.
- Layered arena: floor, overlay, decals, light pools, props behind, perimeter,
  actors, and props in front.
- Deterministic arena decoration layout with non-colliding props.
- Extended arena: `36 x 20` world units.
- Camera follow with smoothing and arena clamping on phone and tablet aspects.
- Spawn ring outside the camera view with a safe farthest-corner fallback.
- Visual-polish fallback: the runtime service and light budget exist, but
  camera post-processing and the global light are disabled for this release.
- ArenaCamera, VisualPolish, top-down rig, atlas, layout, and spawn coverage.

## Known issues and deferred work

- Bloom and vignette are disabled in v0.8.0. Enabling URP post-processing
  produced a beige, low-contrast scene on the Android emulator. Follow-up #203
  tracks the corrected implementation.
- Android evidence is emulator-only. Physical phone and tablet profiling remains
  in #99.
- No human playtest is claimed.
- Procedural audio remains placeholder audio; final authored audio remains in
  v1.0.0.
- The production APK is ARM64-only and is not installed on the x86_64 emulator.
