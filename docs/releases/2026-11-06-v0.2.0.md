- Release date: 2026-11-06

## Summary

Second public version of JabaSurvivol. It turns the walking skeleton into a
round-based run: deterministic round progression, escalating spawn budgets,
round-scaled enemies, persistent round rewards, a round-end selection screen,
and headless balance simulations.

## Included

- Deterministic `Game.Core` round model with start, active, cleared, and failed
  phases, per-round spawn budgets, and round-scaled zombie health, speed, and
  damage.
- HUD round counter with enemies remaining and the persistent highest round
  record.
- Deterministic reward options for supplies, XP, ammo crates, and armor plates.
- Persistent supplies and XP with run-only ammo and armor.
- Safe-area-aware round-end reward selection panel with three touch cards,
  keyboard 1/2/3 shortcuts, profile save, and automatic next-round start.
- Headless balance simulation for rounds 1 to 10 with pacing, economy growth,
  and outlier reporting.
- Android version `0.2.0`, version code `2`.

## Known issues

- Unity CI remains disabled until `UNITY_CI_ENABLED` and the Unity license
  secrets are configured. Issue #27 remains blocked.
- Round 10 clears without a boss encounter or victory state. The boss and MVP
  completion flow remain in later scope.
- Reward amounts are initial balance values. The headless simulation validates
  pacing and income, but it does not replace a human playtest.
- The round-end panel and tablet profile were not captured in the emulator
  session; the panel is covered by PlayMode tests and uses the shared safe-area
  root.
- The production ARM64-only APK still needs a physical ARM device test.
- Runtime art is procedural placeholder art.
- Localization and monetization are intentionally deferred to later milestones.

## Save compatibility

- Save schema version: 1.
- Fresh installs create a new profile.
- Death resets only the current run; supplies, XP, level, skill points, highest
  round, and records persist.
- Existing v0.1.0 profiles remain compatible.
